TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

What this monitor can—and cannot—see.

Beacon Ledger reads BNB Chain public data and scans selected blocks for watched-wallet transfers.

  • Can show: latest public block, wallet balance reads, direct BNB transfers and selected token transfer logs.
  • Cannot show: pending mempool activity, internal signing infrastructure, guaranteed wallet identity, or comprehensive cross-chain fund flows.
  • Not a safety rating: alerts are mechanical threshold checks. Verify evidence independently.
