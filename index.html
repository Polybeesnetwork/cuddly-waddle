TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

What this monitor can—and cannot—see.

Beacon Ledger reads the latest block and public account balances via BNB Chain's public RPC. It checks blocks for direct native BNB transfers to or from addresses you choose while the page remains open.

  • It can show: balance snapshots, direct native-value transactions observed in scanned blocks, and links to public explorer records.
  • It cannot show: internal token transfers without indexed logs, pending mempool transactions, off-chain signing activity, exchange custody internals, or future transactions.
  • No guarantee: an alert is a mechanical rule, not a finding of fraud. RPC outages, reorgs, and polling gaps can cause incomplete observations.
