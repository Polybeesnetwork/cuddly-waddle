# Competitor analysis

Security product positioning matrix and source links are maintained at [`research/Competitor_Security_Matrix.md`](research/Competitor_Security_Matrix.md). Beacon Ledger deliberately complements explorers and threat intelligence rather than claiming to outperform them. Differentiation hypothesis: accessible, versioned campaign expectations next to transaction and milestone evidence. This hypothesis needs user validation.

Crowdfunding analogues (Gitcoin, Juicebox, Kickstarter and GoFundMe) span grant funding, on-chain treasury tools and conventional fundraising. This product does not claim a documented universal shortcoming across them. It narrows scope to BNB project treasury accountability, local-first monitoring and a verifiable chain-to-milestone record; legal and contributor-protection feasibility remains unresolved.
